### The Key Differences Between Traditional IT Support and ITSM

Traditional IT support is often reactive, addressing problems as they arise. In contrast, **ITSM follows a proactive approach**, leveraging structured processes and automation to prevent incidents before they impact operations.

**Key differences:**

- **Proactive vs. Reactive:** ITSM prevents issues before they occur, whereas traditional IT support only reacts to them.
- **Process-Oriented:** ITSM follows structured methodologies such as ITIL (Information Technology Infrastructure Library) to standardize IT operations.
- **Business Alignment:** ITSM ensures that IT services support overall business objectives, rather than just solving technical problems.

### How ServiceNow ITSM Helps Businesses Transform Their IT Operations?
