[ServiceNow IT Service Management (ITSM)](/en/servicenow-itsm/) and ITOM serve different purposes, but together, they transform how organizations manage operations.

### Role of ITSM: Service Delivery

**ITSM** focuses on how organizations **manage services delivered to end users**. It covers incidents, managing service requests, approvals, and change tracking. The goal is to ensure consistent, reliable service experiences.

### Role of ITOM: Infrastructure & Performance

**ITOM**, on the other hand, **manages the underlying infrastructure**. It ensures everything stays healthy, optimized, and automated—so ITSM can deliver on its promises.

When integrated,**ITSM and ITOM offer a complete view**. You can trace a service issue back to its source—and resolve it before it impacts users
