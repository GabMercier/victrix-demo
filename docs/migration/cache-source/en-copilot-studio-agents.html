    Publié le : July 3, 2025


     

    What if we told you that you could enhance the customer and employee experience with an intelligent agent tailored specifically to your organization? 

    When you provide your teams with a custom chatbot, you give them an indispensable tool for improving their efficiency and accuracy — a faithful copilot that speeds up their information searches and supports them in their daily tasks.

    Deploying a tailored intelligent agent means providing your employees with a tool that will improve their productivity in their daily tasks by helping them to be more efficient and accurate with a tool that:

    • Knows your business, its resources, and its processes inside and out
    • Has a foolproof memory
    • Is capable of supporting or executing complex tasks across all your functions
    • Anticipates your needs and easily adapts to new tasks

    FAQ — Agents Intelligents Copilot Studio

    What Is an Intelligent Agent in Artificial Intelligence?

    An intelligent agent in AI is a customized system tailored to your environment and data. These systems are capable of interacting, responding, and acting by designing their own workflows and using existing tools.

    What Is the Difference Between a Tailored Copilot Agent and a Traditional Chatbot?

    Traditional chatbots answer questions. Copilot agents take action.

    Copilot agents trigger workflows, query databases, or connect multiple systems via Microsoft Graph or Power Platform connectors, with little or no human intervention. Traditional chatbots are limited in terms of knowledge and reasoning capabilities without human intervention at every step.

    The Copilot agent is therefore a truly intelligent assistant, capable of interpreting intentions, contextualizing its responses, and providing real operational value.

    Is It Compatible With Dynamics 365 and Microsoft 365?

    Yes. Copilot Studio agents integrate natively with Microsoft 365, including Microsoft Teams, SharePoint, Outlook, Word, and Excel apps. They can also interact with Microsoft Dynamics 365 using Microsoft Graph connectors or Power Automate, depending on your permission settings. A good example: in Dynamics 365 Field Service, for instance, an intelligent scheduling assistant supports your dispatchers in managing field operations.

    But that’s not all – the tailored intelligent agents designed by Victrix also integrate with your other internal systems to search for information or complete tasks directly within them.
