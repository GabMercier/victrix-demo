IT Procurement and Management Services for Your Licenses and Equipment

1

Analysis and Selection of IT Solutions

With Victrix, selecting and purchasing licenses and equipment becomes a simple, secure process aligned with your IT requirements.

Victrix is a value-added reseller for organizations and government agencies. Our experts assess your needs, compare market options, and recommend IT licenses and hardware purchases that perfectly align with your performance and security priorities.

Our approach ensures that your IT environment remains consistent, compliant, and easy to manage.

2

Proactive Renewal and Contract Management

Victrix handles the management and renewal of contracts and licenses.

Our IT asset management service covers:

  • • Monitoring of expiration dates
  • • Contract renegotiation
  • • License compliance
  • • Analysis of subscription or purchase options based on your priorities
 

Our approach ensures your business continuity, compliance, and cost-effective, predictable IT expenses.

3

Support, Integration, and Deployment

With Victrix, benefit from end-to-end support for the deployment of your IT solutions.

Our experts ensure seamless integration within your existing environments, working closely with your internal teams and technology partners.

After go live, we provide support, warranty management, upgrades, and technical monitoring to maintain the performance and compliance of your systems.
