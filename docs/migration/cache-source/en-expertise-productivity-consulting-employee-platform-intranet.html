The Platform for a Unified, Modern Employee Experience 

Victrix helps you build an internal communications tool that goes far beyond the traditional intranet. By leveraging the power of Microsoft's artificial intelligence, we transform your digital environment into a fluid, engaging experience.

The pillars of the Victrix Employee Platform offering:

  • Comprehensive and accessible digital environment:

Designing navigation and architecture that takes into account the employee's entire digital workspace

  • Simplified information retrieval and knowledge sharing:

Bridging the gap between the user and the information they are seeking

  • Personalized experience:

Journeys and content tailored to different user segments

Benefits of Our Modern Intranet Offering

Victrix offers more than a simple corporate intranet solution. We develop a bespoke platform designed to boost productivity and collaboration, powered by AI and perfectly tailored to your organization's needs. We offer:

  • Proven expertise in Microsoft 365 solutions (Teams, SharePoint, Copilot, etc.) and Viva
  • An intelligent chatbot, integrated into your work environment to answer employees' common questions
  • A flexible, scalable offering tailored to your digital strategy
  • A dedicated team of experts in user experience (UX), interface design (UI), and communication
  • Extensive experience within complex environments and private or public sector organizations

 

With Victrix's productivity experts by your side, improving the employee experience becomes an accessible and effective reality.
