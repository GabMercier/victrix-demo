Law 25: Is Your Organization Compliant?

With the new provisions of Law 25 now in effect, compliance is not only mandatory, it is a matter of credibility and security for your organization. Given the risk of penalties and your customers’ heightened expectations, compliance is now inextricably linked to your reputation.

Ensure Law 25 compliance with Victrix

Assess your organization's compliance by answering these key questions:

"*" indicates required fields

1. Leadership and transparency: Have you designated a Privacy Officer and published his/her contact details as well as your privacy and security policy on your website?*
2. Clear consent: Do your personal data collection methods maintain and respect the consent guidelines established by law, and do you provide clear information on the purposes and means of collection?*
3. Contracts with suppliers: Have you audited and adjusted your agreements with service providers to include protections for personal data, especially for data that crosses borders, and have you assessed the risks to privacy?*
4. Preparation and incident response: Do you have a rapid response plan in the event of a security or confidentiality incident, do you keep a confidentiality incident log, and have you implemented cybersecurity awareness measures for your employees?*
5. Innovation and privacy: Do your technologies incorporate privacy principles right from the design stage, and do they ensure, by default, the protection of user data in terms of identification, localization and profiling, without requiring user intervention?*
