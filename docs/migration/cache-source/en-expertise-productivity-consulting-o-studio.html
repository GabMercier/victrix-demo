FAQ — Dynamics 365 & Microsoft Power Platform Development

Does Ø Studio Offer Mobile Application Development?

Yes. Our Microsoft application development services consider accessibility from mobile devices and cybersecurity as core components of the app development process. If you want a web application designed and developed with Microsoft Power Platform or Dynamics 365, we can help.

What Are the Steps to Creating an Application?

Proper planning before design is essential, whether it’s heavy-code development or no-code/low-code app creation. Here are the 5 steps to successful Dynamics 365 and Microsoft Power Platform application development:

  • Define objectives: identify and quantify business needs
  • Review requirements and user behavior, and identify key users
  • Present a demo or proof of value
  • Analyze gaps between requirements and the demo or proof of value
  • Present findings and an implementation proposal
