Protect Your Entire Digital Workspace, Network, Exposure, and Use of AI with a Unified Cybersecurity Architecture

Secure the full spectrum of cybersecurity with Check Point technologies distributed by Victrix. Each solution integrates easily with your IT environment and strengthens your organization’s security posture. 

Hybrid Mesh

Protect your infrastructure and ensure compliance across your data centres, cloud environments, and interconnections. 

Formerly known as Network Security, Quantum Security, and Cloud Guard, Hybrid Mesh is a unique architecture that combines unified control, centralized policies, and distributed deployment from data centres to the cloud, branches, and remote users.

Result: consistent data protection, enhanced regulatory compliance, and simplified network perimeter management. 

Workspace Security Platform

Protect your employees, their devices, their tools, and their communications. 

Workspace Security Platform is an alliance of Harmony solutions (Endpoint, Email and collaboration, etc.) designed to protect users, endpoints, browsers, collaborative tools and employees' use of AI.

Result: a secure experience across the entire digital workspace for your teams, without compromising productivity. 

Exposure Management

Protect your attack surface, limit your exposure, and automate remediation.

Check Point Exposure Management combines Cyberint, Infinity ERM, Threat Intelligence Services, and more to deliver external and internal exposure management, threat intelligence, dark web monitoring, risk prioritization, and automated remediation via Veriti.

Result: controlled and effective exposure management that reduces your attack surface and limits your vulnerabilities.

AI Security

Ensure the secure use of artificial intelligence within your organization.

AI Security is the merger of Check Point's ThreadCloud AI and Infinity AI solutions. It is a central component of your company's cybersecurity. It secures employees' use of generative AI tools, protects AI applications, enhances the security of autonomous AI agents, and prevents data leaks via prompts and models.

Result : enhanced protection for the use of artificial intelligence, from generative AI to AI apps and autonomous agents.

Access and Identity

Apply Zero Trust principles across your entire organization.   

CheckPoint SASE (Secure Access solutions) control every connection and enforce least privilege based on risk level. 

Result: controlled access to critical data and applications wherever your users are located. 

Monitoring and Operations 

Centralize threat detection, analysis and response.  

Horizon provides full visibility into your environments and automates incident response. 

Result: Faster, more coordinated, and effective security operations.
