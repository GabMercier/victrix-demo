YOUR CHALLENGES

AI Is Already in Your Organization. Now Make It Deliver.

The enthusiasm is real, but adoption is moving ahead with no direction, no coordination, and no sign-off. That leaves your organization to contend with:

  • Growing requests for tools and licenses, with no approval process.
  • Little visibility into what your teams are already doing with AI.
  • Scattered initiatives that are hard to prioritize and poorly aligned with your business goals.
  • Inflated expectations that mistake quick wins for lasting transformations.
  • Governance still to be defined before investing further.

"The question isnt' whether organizations should use AI anymore. Most are already using it, but with a huge blind spot: they've often left the optimization of their processes in the hands of individuals or small teams, which limits visibility into concrete gains.

They often proceed haphazardly, launching numerous initiatives without governance, without prioritization, and without always targeting the use cases that will create the most value. Or worse yet, by making the wrong technological choices to implement them.

You don't tackle a critical, standardized business process with a mainstream chatbot and a few random prompts."


Olivier Maltais

Director of Innovative Digital Solutions, Victrix
