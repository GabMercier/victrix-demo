What Victrix's SEvOC Has to Offer

Continuous monitoring, accelerated incident response, and enhanced cybersecurity. 24/7, bilingual SOC service with experts dedicated to your environment.

Accelerate your cybersecurity and optimize all your security tools. Unlike a traditional SOC, our scalable, modular offering keeps you at the forefront of security technologies.

Benefits of Our SOC Service

➜ Deal with the increase in advanced cyber threats (ransomware, targeted attacks, attacks undetected by conventional approaches)

➜ Respond rapidly to security alerts

➜ Supplement your in-house security team with our experts to ensure the evolution and maintenance of your tools (EDR, SIEM, CSPM)

➜ Simplify the integration of your new entities in a context of growth or merger

➜ Achieve your strategic objectives while ensuring cybersecurity

What SEvOC Brings to Your Organization

SUPPORT

• Seamless integration into your existing tools

• Management of your security equipment

• Guidance in crisis management and remediation

• Governance and production of strategic operational indicators

• Evolution roadmap

OPERATIONS

• 24/7/365 monitoring of security alerts, operated exclusively by bilingual security analysts

• Implementation of a Zero Trust approach: systematic control of identity, behavior, and access

• Vulnerability management

• Configuration, maintenance, intelligent automation, and orchestration: controlled use of AI and orchestration via automation tools, with human supervision at every stage

• Monitoring the threat landscape targeting your organization (threat intelligence)

• Analysis, triage, and response to security incidents

EVOLUTION

• Updates, enhancements, and consolidation

• Distinctive approach to mergers and acquisitions (M&A): accelerated securing of acquired environments, with the creation of child tenants under the control of the parent SOC
