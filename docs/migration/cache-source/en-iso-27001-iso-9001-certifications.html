    Victrix Renews Its ISO 27001 and ISO 9001 Certifications: a Double Seal of Excellence and Trust

    Publié le : July 29, 2025

    A Renewed Commitment to Quality and Information Security

     

    MONTREAL, JULY. 23, 2025 — Victrix is pleased to announce the renewal of its ISO 27001 and ISO 9001 certifications, reaffirming its unwavering commitment to information security and excellence in quality management.

    This dual international recognition consolidates Victrix’s position as a Quebec leader in the field of information technology.

    ISO 27001 Certification: Information Security at the Heart of Our Priorities

    The globally recognized ISO/IEC 27001 standard provides a rigorous framework for information security management, cybersecurity, and privacy protection. Obtaining and maintaining this certification requires a robust management system, a vigilant organizational culture and an ongoing commitment to improving security processes.

    At Victrix, this approach translates into constant investment in risk prevention, the adoption of best practices, and proactive protection of our customers’ sensitive data.

    ISO 9001 Certification: Quality Drives Innovation and Trust

    Renewed for the 21st consecutive year on July 19th, 2025, ISO 9001 certification attests to Victrix’s ability to structure and optimize its internal processes to provide impeccable service to its customers, with a focus on continuous improvement.

    This benchmark standard applies to the entire value chain, from needs analysis to the implementation of high-performance IT solutions, and guarantees an exceptional customer experience based on rigor, reliability, and attentiveness.

    Recognized Expertise Serving Local Organizations

    Since its founding in 2003, Victrix has been supporting Canada’s largest companies in their digital transformation and information asset security projects.

    Its reputation is based on cutting-edge expertise, the commitment of its teams, and a rigorous professional ethic. The ISO 27001 and ISO 9001 certifications, obtained after exhaustive audit processes, are tangible proof of the added value offered to its customers and partners.

    A Word from our CEO

    “These certifications are much more than international standards: they are a concrete expression of the trust we inspire in our clients and partners and the professionalism of our teams. At Victrix, we firmly believe that operational excellence and information security are integral to a lasting and transparent business relationship.”

    Dominic Lajoie
    CEO, Victrix
