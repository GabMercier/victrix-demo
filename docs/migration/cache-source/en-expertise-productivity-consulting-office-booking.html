O bureau, Our Office Booking Application

Discover our office booking application. Easy to use and set up, our app will help you manage your office space in no time!

Request a demo

BENEFITS

Simplify reservations

Improve office management

Access relevant reports

Track office attendance

A SIMPLE, ERGONOMIC APPLICATION TO MANAGE YOUR WORKSPACES

For employees

  • Reserve an office according to your needs
  • Change, cancel or confirm your reservation
  • Create a recurring reservation
  • Link the reservation to your Outlook calendar
  • Confirm your presence with a unique office code
  • View your colleagues' reservations and select an office near them
  • Designate a colleague to delegate reservations

For office managers

  • Create and manage offices, zones, floors, and buildings
  • Manage specific features and equipment available for an office
  • Manage office and zone availability
  • View reports on space utilization, busy periods, and employee attendance

MICROSOFT TECHNOLOGY

O bureau integrates with your Microsoft 365 and Power Platform environment

THE BENEFITS OF OUR OFFICE RESERVATION APPLICATION INCLUDE

  • One time setup cost
  • Full visibility for informed decision-making regarding the management of your workspace
  • Rapid deployment
  • Native integration with Microsoft Teams
  • Autonomous management and evolution of the solution
  • Bilingual app, french/english
