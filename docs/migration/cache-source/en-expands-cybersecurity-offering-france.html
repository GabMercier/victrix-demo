    Victrix expands its cybersecurity offering in France

    Publié le : September 16, 2024

    MONTREAL, SEPTEMBER 04, 2024 — Victrix expands its cybersecurity offering in France

    A new step to support your digital transformation

    By joining forces with COMITEM, Victrix is expanding its offering in France as part of the strong branding strategy launched by Alan Allman Associates.  

    This new step marks a significant advance in our international growth strategy and demonstrates our commitment to supporting our customers in their digital transformation challenges. 

    An end-to-end cybersecurity offering to meet your needs

    At Victrix, we offer a comprehensive suite of services to help organizations meet the ever-increasing challenges posed by digital threats.

    Our offering is organized around three main service lines, each of which plays a key role in protecting, optimizing and evolving our customers' technology environments.

    1. Consulting and Strategy: Defining a 360° vision of cybersecurity that is tailored to the specific needs of each organization. The goal is to provide a strategic framework that guides short-, medium-, and long-term actions while ensuring effective security governance.
    2. Tactical Support: Deploying the technologies and processes that strengthen your security posture. Tactical support ensures that each organization has the technical and human resources to quickly adapt to new challenges.
    3. Operational Management: Helping our customers manage the day-to-day security of their systems through continuous monitoring, proactive maintenance, and regular updates. This approach ensures that systems remain robust in the face of emerging threats.

    Are you ready to take on the challenges of cybersecurity?

    Want to learn more about our offerings in France? Don't hesitate to contact us. Our teams are at your disposal to answer all your questions.
