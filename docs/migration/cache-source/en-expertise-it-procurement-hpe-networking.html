Standardize Your Network Architecture and Simplify Operations with HPE Networking 

HPE Networking solutions combine HPE Aruba and HPE Juniper to address connectivity, security, and governance requirements. Benefit from a cohesive network portfolio designed to scale with your organization.

Data Center Interconnection

Optimize the performance and resilience of your data centers.

Juniper PTX, MX and QFX series solutions deliver interconnection, routing, and switching for high-density environments. They are designed to handle traffic growth and increasing workloads while supporting expansion without major architectural redesign.

Result: a stable, scalable data center infrastructure suited for critical environments.

Network Experience and Operations

Réduce resolution times and improve visibility with operational intelligence (AIOps).

Mist AI and the Marvis artificial intelligence virtual assistant continuously analyze network performance, detect anomalies, and recommend corrective actions. They help anticipate issues and streamline daily management.

Result: more efficient operations and an improved experience for IT teams and users.

Firewalls

Protect your environments from the edge to the data center.

Juniper SRX firewalls enhance security across data centers, remote sites, and strategic access points. They provide traffic control, segmentation, and consistent enforcement of security policies.

Result: security integrated into your network architecture without adding operational complexity. 

Wireless Access

Deploy reliable and secure Wi-Fi connectivity across all your sites. 

Juniper wireless access solutions provide stable coverage and centralized management, with continuous visibility into performance and user experience. They integrate with existing security policies.

Result: a consistent and secure user experience from campus to branch locations. 

Switches and Wired Access

Standardize LAN connectivity across your sites.

Juniper EX series switches provide access and aggregation for campus and branch environments. They enable consistent management and segmentation aligned with security requirements.

Result: a stable, secure, and easy-to-use wired architecture.

Switches 

Modernize and simplify the management of your LAN infrastructure.

Aruba CX switches provide access, aggregation, and core networking capabilities with built-in automation and segmentation. They enable secure, consistent management and improved traffic visibility.

Result: a high-performing, stable wired infrastructure that is easier to manage.

Wireless Access

Deliver reliable and secure Wi-Fi connectivity across all your locations.

Aruba access points provide high-performance coverage in indoor and outdoor environments. They integrate with centralized management and consistent security policies.

Result: a stable and consistent user experience across primary and remote sites.

Management and Observability

Centralize administration and gain full visibility across your network.

HPE Aruba Networking Central enables management of switches, Wi-Fi, and WAN through a unified interface. The platform provides observability and automation capabilities to quickly detect anomalies and optimize performance.

Result: simplified governance and comprehensive visibility across your network environments.

WAN and Remote Sites

Optimize connectivity between your locations and cloud environments.

EdgeConnect orchestrates traffic, prioritizes critical applications, and secures communications between headquarters, branches, and the cloud. The solution ensuares efficient and secure connectivity across sites and cloud environments.

Result: an agile, high-performing, and secure WAN adapted to multi-site realities.

Secure Access

Control and secure every network connection.

ClearPass Policy Manager enforces access policies based on user identity, device type, and risk level. SASE and Zero Trust capabilities extend protection to remote access and cloud applications.

Result: consistent access control and a strengthened security posture across the entire network.
