Cybersecurity in the Healthcare Sector

Victrix offers a customized cybersecurity service for the healthcare sector. Protect patient data, your connected medical devices, and your information systems against all cyber threats.

 

Strengthen your cybersecurity

 

Cybersecurity Challenges in the Healthcare Sector

 

1

Governance and Resilience

  • Preparing healthcare facilities to deal with cyber threats
  • Being able to conduct a cyber risk self-assessment*
2

Resources and Sharing

  • Identify and increase the number of professionals
  • Ensure sufficient budget to strengthen cybersecurity and digital technology
  • Develop or find a cybersecurity service for healthcare institutions tailored to specific and priority needs
3

Raising Awareness of Cybersecurity in Healthcare

  • Raise awareness among senior management
  • Raise awareness and train all employees
  • Implement cybersecurity crisis drills in case of phishing attacks or other threats*
4

Operational Security

  • Secure facility directories (AD)* and medical equipment (IoT)
  • Manage exposure risks and access
  • Develop a business continuity and disaster recovery plan

+ Agency

Digital Health Agency (ANS – FR) Digital Health Record (QC) Quebec Health Agency

+ Ministry

Quebec Ministry of Health and Social Services CISSS (integrated health and social services center) + regional variations

+ Integrated University Centers

+ Private Establishments

Victrix Provides First-Class Cybersecurity and Confidentiality for Healthcare Organizations

Victrix has extensive experience in the healthcare sector. Our cybersecurity consultants understand the challenges healthcare providers face on a daily basis. We use cybersecurity best practices to protect your personal information and strengthen the security of your healthcare facility's systems.

 

1

Strategy & Governance

Protect patient safety and personal data with our strategic approach. We identify your assets, analyze your risk, and ensure compliance to elevate the security of your sensitive medical data.
2

IT Modernization

Let our privacy experts help you counter cyber threats and stay ahead of technological advances. We accelerate your digital transformation through virtualization, automation, and strategic orchestration. Benefit from enhanced perimeter security with Zero Trust architecture, tailored hybrid cloud solutions, and highly secure networks.
3

Cloud & Application Optimization

Take advantage of cloud cybersecurity services including secure infrastructure deployment (PaaS, SaaS, and IaaS), application transformation and optimization (DevOps/DevSecOps), process automation, and API security.
4

Tactical and Operational Support

Get the support you need to assess your security posture and maturity, deploy controls (EDR-XDR-MDR), and manage identities and access (IDaaS). We also support you in operational management with cybersecurity audits and 24/7 managed services.
5

Cyber Resilience

Ensure your cyber resilience through customized cybersecurity solutions such as backup and archiving, email security, and regular updates to your IT security tools. We take care of the security of your infrastructure, network, and work environments, and offer our expertise in crisis management and cyber insurance.
