    Dominic Lajoie Appointed COO and CIO of Victrix

    Publié le : November 14, 2024

    MONTRÉAL, NOVEMBER, 14 — Dominic Lajoie Appointed COO and CIO of Victrix: A Milestone for Our Growth

    We are excited to announce the appointment of Dominic Lajoie as Chief Operating Officer (COO) and Chief Information Officer (CIO) of Victrix. In this exclusive interview, Victrix President Stéphan Gariépy discusses Dominic's motivations, strategic vision, and ambitions for the company's future.

    Seizing a Unique Opportunity for Growth

     

    Stéphan Gariépy: Dominic, we are thrilled to welcome you to the Victrix team. To start, what motivated you to take on this challenge as our COO/CIO?

    Dominic Lajoie: Thank you, Stéphan. I accepted this position because I saw an exceptional opportunity with Victrix to support a growing company with well-defined ambitions. What particularly attracted me was the possibility of bringing my experience in digital transformation and growth management. I am convinced that my skills in structuring and implementing processes can effectively support Victrix's goals.

     

    Strategic Expertise for the Next Phase of Growth

     

    Stéphan Gariépy: You have already led companies through phases of accelerated growth. What specific strengths do you think you can bring to our team for this new stage of development?

    Dominic Lajoie: Indeed, my career has allowed me to manage rapid growth and structure organizations to make them more agile and efficient. At Victrix, I believe my role will be to consolidate processes while maintaining the flexibility to respond quickly to new opportunities. As COO and CIO, I will focus on operational excellence, talent development, and expanding our expertise, particularly in Artificial Intelligence, ServiceNow, and cybersecurity.

     

    The Importance of AI, ServiceNow, and Cybersecurity

     

    Stéphan Gariépy: You mentioned AI, ServiceNow, and cybersecurity. Why do you believe these areas are critical to Victrix's future?

    Dominic Lajoie: These areas of expertise have become crucial for any company wanting to remain competitive. Artificial Intelligence is transforming how businesses manage their operations, ServiceNow enables smooth and efficient IT service management, and cybersecurity has become a constant concern as threats evolve rapidly. For Victrix, mastering these skills allows us to deliver unique added value to our clients and position ourselves as a top-tier strategic partner.

     

    A Vision for Regional Growth and Expansion

     

    Stéphan Gariépy: How do you plan to implement this vision of growth and expansion into new regions?

    Dominic Lajoie: My plan is to build a solid foundation that supports sustainable expansion. This means first investing in the internal development of our teams and recruiting experts in each of these areas. Then, we will continue to target strategic partnerships and explore opportunities in markets where we have a limited presence. The goal is to establish Victrix as a benchmark player, not only in Quebec but also in other regions.

     

    Ambitions for the Future

     

    Stéphan Gariépy: One last question, Dominic. What do you hope to accomplish for Victrix in the coming years?
    Dominic Lajoie: I hope to see Victrix emerge as a leader in technology innovation and cybersecurity in new regions. My ambition is to help build an agile organization that can quickly adapt to market changes and meet our clients' growing needs in security, innovation, and efficiency. My vision is to make Victrix a reference not only for its technological solutions but also for its ability to anticipate and respond to the challenges of modern businesses.

     

    A Key Step for Victrix

    Stéphan Gariépy: Thank you, Dominic. I’m confident that your arrival marks a pivotal moment for Victrix. With your expertise and vision, we’re ready to tackle tomorrow’s challenges and achieve our ambitious growth objectives.

     

    We are thrilled to welcome Dominic Lajoie to the team and look forward to building the future of Victrix together.
