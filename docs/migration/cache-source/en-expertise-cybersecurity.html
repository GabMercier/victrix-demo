Complete List of Our Cybersecurity Offerings for Organizations

Cybersecurity is part of Victrix’s DNA. We help private and public sector organizations achieve their information security objectives through strategic, value-added support.

Our cybersecurity services include:

  • Penetration Testing (Pentest)
  • GRC Audit (Risk and Compliance Management)
  • Business Continuity and Recovery Plans (BCP/BRP)
  • Implementation of the Zero Trust Model
  • Compliance with Law 25, GDPR, DORA, and other personal data protection standards
  • IoT security and inventory assessment
  • Identity Management and Access Control with the SASE model

What Does Victrix Do to Ensure Operational Security?

Victrix supports public and private sector cybersecurity efforts through security posture and maturity assessments, deployment of controls (EDR-XDR-MDR), identity and access management (IDaaS), and operational management, including cybersecurity audits and 24/7 managed services.

Can Victrix Help My Organization Secure Its Cloud?

Victrix is your strategic partner for cloud cybersecurity services such as secure infrastructure deployment (PaaS, SaaS, and IaaS), application transformation and optimization (DevOps/DevSecOps), API protection, and process automation.
