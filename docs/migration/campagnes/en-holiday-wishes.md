- **h2** — Playing «<em>Le jeu de la boulette</em>» — Fun for Everyone!
- **p** — Nothing beats a moment of humour and humility quite like miming a famous character in front of your team.
- **p** — <strong>«Le jeu de la boulette» is simple.</strong> Each participant writes down three well-known characters (fictional or real) on small pieces of paper, then crumples them into little balls. Each round, a player from each team draws a boulette and has one minute to make their team guess the character, uncrumpling included.
- **p** — The game unfolds in three rounds:
- **li** — <strong>Round 1:</strong> Say anything you want... except the character's name.
- **li** — <strong>Round 2: </strong>You can only use one word.
- **li** — <strong>Round 3: </strong>No words at all, only mime!
- **p** — A round ends once all the characters have been guessed. Each correct answer is worth one point.
- **p** — A suggestion from <a href="https://www.linkedin.com/in/benoitchampagne/">Benoit Champagne</a><a href="https://www.linkedin.com/in/benoitchampagne/"> </a>
